# October

**A visual agent runtime and agents infrastructure company.**

We build the pieces that let AI agents leave the chat window: use your car, your phone, your printer and your hardware; run side by side with you on your machine; and reach any model without giving away your keys or your identity.

[october.dev](https://www.october.dev)

## Agents in the real world

| | |
| --- | --- |
| [**car-use**](https://github.com/october-dev/car-use) | Let your agent use your car. Read and diagnose real cars over OBD-II, or a built-in virtual car. |
| [**november**](https://github.com/october-dev/november) | An agentic harness for hardware. Sensors, switches and small boards, with safety checks the agent can't get around. |
| [**printer-use**](https://github.com/october-dev/printer-use) | Let your agent use your printer. Plain-English printing over CUPS, as a desktop app and a CLI. |
